## The Framework: Four Phases

Every Maru engagement follows four phases. No jargon, no mystery. You know exactly where you are at every stage, what we're doing, and what you'll receive.

---

## Phase 1: Diagnose

**What happens:** We learn your business, find where time and money are being wasted, and identify where AI will have the biggest impact on your costs — starting with your website.

**How it works:**

We start with a free 30-minute discovery call. No pitch, no pressure — we listen. We want to understand how your business operates day to day: where your team spends their time, what tools you're already using, what frustrates you, and where things fall through the cracks.

After that call, we send you a short brief to fill in. It captures the details we need about your operations, your tools, your team structure, and your goals. You fill it in at your own pace.

Once we have your brief, we do the heavy lifting. We run a full diagnostic on your business — and that starts with your website.

**Your website is infrastructure, not a brochure.** It's the front door of every automated workflow we build. If it's slow, broken, or invisible to Google, nothing we build behind it will perform. Most business websites were built pre-COVID as digital brochures and haven't been touched since. They're slow on mobile, missing sitemaps, have broken links, and aren't connected to anything. We see this in almost every engagement — and it's the first thing we fix.

Our diagnostic covers two layers:

*Site Infrastructure Assessment:* We audit your website's performance — speed, mobile experience, SEO fundamentals, broken links, security, and whether it's actually connected to your business tools or just sitting there. You'll see a clear Site Health Score and a plain-language breakdown of what's costing you visitors, leads, and credibility.

*Workflow & Process Assessment:* We map your current workflows — how leads come in, how your team handles them, where data moves manually between tools, and where things fall through the cracks. We identify the specific manual processes that are eating your team's time and calculate what they're costing you.

This is where our own AI systems earn their keep: the same kind of workflow automation we build for clients is what powers our research and analysis internally.

**What you get:**

A Diagnostic Report that shows you, in plain language:
- Your Site Health Score — how your website is performing as infrastructure (speed, mobile, SEO, broken elements) and what it's costing your business
- Where your team is losing time to manual, repetitive work
- What those inefficiencies are costing you (in hours and in Rands)
- Which workflows would benefit most from AI-powered automation
- Whether your website needs remediation before automation will deliver results
- A prioritised list of opportunities, ranked by impact and ease of implementation

**Timeline:** 5–10 working days from brief submission.

**Your investment:** The discovery call is free. The Diagnostic Report is included in the project scope — you only pay if you decide to proceed to Phase 2.

---

## Phase 2: Design

**What happens:** We map the solution. You see exactly what we'll build, how it connects to your existing tools, and what results to expect — before we write a line of code.

**How it works:**

Using the Diagnostic Report as our foundation, we design the AI-powered workflows that will replace your most costly manual processes. This isn't a generic template — it's built around how your business actually operates.

If the diagnostic flagged website infrastructure issues, the Implementation Plan addresses these first. There's no point automating lead follow-up if your website loads in 8 seconds and visitors bounce before they see your offer. We'll scope the remediation — what needs fixing, what it costs, and why it has to happen before the automation layer will deliver results. Some clients choose to have us fix the site; others address it independently. Either way, we won't build on a broken foundation just to collect a fee.

We map each workflow end to end: what triggers it, what the AI handles, what your team still does, and where the data flows. We select the right tools for the job — prioritising tools you already own and pay for, and only adding new ones where there's a clear cost justification.

We present this to you as an Implementation Plan. We walk you through it together. You ask questions, push back, request changes. Nothing gets built until you're satisfied it makes sense for your business.

**What you get:**

An Implementation Plan that includes:
- Workflow maps showing exactly what changes and what stays the same
- Tool recommendations (with reasoning — why this tool, not that one)
- Integration design — how your existing tools will connect and talk to each other
- Expected outcomes: time saved, cost reduced, errors eliminated
- Project timeline with clear milestones
- Fixed-scope investment — no surprises, no scope creep

**Timeline:** 5–7 working days after Diagnostic Report sign-off.

**Decision point:** You review the plan. If it makes sense, we proceed. If not, you walk away with the Diagnostic Report and the Implementation Plan — both are yours to keep.

---

## Phase 3: Build

**What happens:** We build and connect the AI-powered workflows, test them with your team, and make sure everything works in your real environment with your real data.

**How it works:**

If website remediation is in scope, that's where we start. We fix the foundation first — speed, mobile performance, broken links, sitemaps, SEO fundamentals — so that everything built on top of it actually works. This isn't a redesign; it's infrastructure repair. Once the site is performing, we move to the workflow automation layer.

We build in short, focused sprints. You see progress as it happens, not after three months of silence. We use the same AI-powered development tools we recommend to our clients — our build process practices what we preach.

Each workflow is built, tested against real scenarios, and validated before we move to the next. We don't build everything and hope it works — we build one workflow, prove it works, then build the next.

Your team is involved throughout. Not because we need them to do our job, but because the workflows need to fit how your people actually work. We train as we build — by the time we're done, your team isn't learning a new system from scratch. They've been using it for weeks.

**What you get:**

- Working AI-powered workflows, live in your environment
- All integrations connected and tested
- Documentation your team can actually use (not a 50-page manual nobody reads)
- Team training completed during the build, not bolted on at the end

**Timeline:** 2–6 weeks depending on scope. Agreed in the Implementation Plan.

---

## Phase 4: Launch & Measure

**What happens:** We go live, measure the actual results against your baseline, and prove the savings.

**How it works:**

Launch isn't the end — it's the moment we start proving the work. We monitor the new workflows in their first 2–4 weeks of live operation, catching edge cases, fixing anything that doesn't perform as expected, and making sure your team is confident.

More importantly, we measure. We agreed on baseline numbers in Phase 2 — hours spent on manual tasks, error rates, response times, cost per process. Now we measure the same things with the new system running. The comparison is the proof.

We compile this into a Results Report and walk you through it. You see exactly what changed, by how much, and what it means for your operating costs.

**What you get:**

A Results Report showing:
- Before vs. after comparison on every metric we agreed to track
- Actual time saved per week/month
- Estimated annual cost saving in Rands
- Any issues identified and how they were resolved
- Recommendations for what to optimise or expand next

**Timeline:** Results Report delivered 30 days after launch.

**This is where the relationship is either proven or it isn't.** If the numbers show the system is delivering, we've earned the right to discuss ongoing support. If they don't, we fix it — that's included.

---

## Phase 5 (Optional): Optimise

**What happens:** Monthly support to keep the systems performing, expand to new areas, and adapt as your business evolves.

**How it works:**

AI systems aren't static. Your business changes, your tools update, your team grows. The workflows that saved you 15 hours a week in April might need tuning by October. Phase 5 is a monthly retainer that keeps everything running and improving.

This isn't a "pay us to be available" arrangement. Each month includes a defined scope of work: performance monitoring, system health checks, minor adjustments, and a monthly check-in to discuss what's working and what could work better.

If the data shows a new opportunity — a process in another part of the business that's ripe for automation — we flag it. You decide whether to pursue it. No upsell pressure, just evidence.

**What you get:**

- Monthly performance monitoring and system health check
- Minor adjustments and optimisations included
- Monthly check-in call (30 minutes)
- Priority response for issues
- Quarterly review with updated metrics and recommendations

**Investment:** Monthly retainer, agreed at the end of Phase 4. Typical range: R3,500–R14,500/month depending on scope and complexity. No long-term lock-in — month-to-month after an initial 3-month commitment.

## What Makes This Different

**Your website is infrastructure, not a brochure.** Every AI workflow we build runs through your website. If it's slow, broken, or invisible to search engines, nothing we automate behind it will perform. We diagnose the foundation first — and fix it before we build on it. Most consultancies skip this and wonder why the automation doesn't deliver.

**We measure everything.** Most consultancies deliver a project and disappear. We deliver a Results Report that proves the savings. If it doesn't save you money, we haven't done our job.

**We build with what you have.** We don't sell tools. We make your existing tools work together. If you need a new tool, we explain why and what it costs before we add it.

**We use AI to build AI workflows.** The same approach we recommend to clients is how we run our own consultancy. Our research, analysis, and project management are AI-powered. If it works for us, we know it works.

**Fixed scope, no surprises.** You know the investment before we start building. No hourly billing, no scope creep, no "this will cost extra" conversations halfway through.

**We won't build on a broken foundation.** If your website needs fixing before automation will deliver, we'll tell you — and we'll scope the fix honestly. We'd rather lose a deal than build something that won't perform.

### Gaps to Close

The current internal process is strong from brief to proposal (Steps 1–8). The delivery methodology (Step 9 onwards — "open project in Claude Code") needs the same level of codification. Specifically:

- **Sprint structure:** Define standard sprint length, check-in cadence, and deliverable format for Phase 3
- **Training protocol:** Document how team training is integrated into the build process
- **Measurement framework:** Create a standard baseline/measurement template for Phase 4 Results Reports
- **Handover checklist:** Define what "done" looks like — documentation, access, training, sign-off

These should be built as Notion templates in Da-Vi as the first two client engagements progress. Codify from reality, not theory.

01 About

Jimmy Motsei is the Founder and CEO of Maru Online — an AI integration consultancy that helps SMEs replace manual operational bottlenecks with intelligent, automated workflows.

His approach to AI integration is grounded in over two decades as a commercial operator. The business behind Maru Online was originally registered in 2002 as Maru Sponsorship & Marketing — later Maru Sport — where it held the commercial rights to the South African Open Golf Championship for seven consecutive editions and managed complex multi-stakeholder delivery across sport, entertainment, and government. That foundation shapes how he engages with clients today: the question is always what the solution produces for the business, not what the technology is capable of in the abstract.

02 Areas of Focus
AI Workflow Integration
Mapping existing business processes and replacing manual steps with AI-assisted workflows — intake automation, internal knowledge retrieval, client communication, and reporting.
Intelligent Client-Facing Tools
Custom web applications built for business function: diagnostic tools, membership platforms, onboarding flows, and lead qualification systems. Mobile-first, lean-team operable.
Operations Automation
Connecting CRM, email, invoicing, and project management so they operate on real business triggers — not manual data entry. Closes the gap between what a business pays for and what it actually uses.
AI Adoption in Emerging Markets
Practical frameworks for introducing AI to SMEs operating in markets where most owners do not yet identify AI as a solution to their problems — leading with operational pain, not technology.
